Step 1 · Concept

Automated advising inside the display ad

This walkthrough shows how an HTML5 creative can qualify prospects for graduate programmes without leaving the banner: profile, recommendation, and conversion in the same ad space.

The advisor, inside the ad

Not a separate landing page

The user answers three guided questions inside the creative itself (300×600 or 970×250). The catalogue narrows in real time to one or more programmes, showing why each option fits, with score and eligibility.

  • Degree and professional profile
  • Learning goal
  • Preferred study mode

Core engine

Guided sequence + catalogue

Answers trigger rules over a structured programme catalogue. The system filters out incompatible programmes, weights fit, and never invents qualifications that do not exist.

Optional AI layer

Explanation and Q&A

Artificial intelligence can explain the match or answer questions, always grounded in approved catalogue facts. It does not replace admission rules.
