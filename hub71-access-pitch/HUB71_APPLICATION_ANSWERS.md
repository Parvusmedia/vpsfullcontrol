## What are you building and how is it different… Solution and UVP (+ IP) *
*(200 word limit)*

NextConvers is an AI decision layer that sits **before** outreach. We identify B2B prospects, score affinity 1–5 stars using role, seniority, company attributes, industry and geo fit, then sync prioritized profiles into the CRM and sequencers teams already use. We do **not** send messages—we decide who to contact, in what order, with what priority.

**Differentiation vs market**
- Enrichment tools (Apollo, ZoomInfo) provide contacts and filters; they do not prioritize daily “who first” for the sales motion.
- Sequencers (Smartlead, etc.) automate sending; they amplify both good and bad lists.
- CRMs are systems of record, not ICP affinity engines.
- NextConvers is the missing prioritization layer: plug-and-play scoring that feeds the existing stack in minutes.

**Unique value:** higher acceptance/reply quality, less SDR waste, clearer focus for agencies and growth teams—without replacing tools they already pay for. Adjacent wedge: DataForMedia (LinkedIn audience intelligence) reinforces the same signal thesis.

**IP:** No registered patents to date. Proprietary scoring logic, data pipelines and product IP are company-owned trade secrets / software copyright (confirm counsel wording if needed).
