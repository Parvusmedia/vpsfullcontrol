Who it's for

Built for teams that start from a legal name or incomplete CRM row and need a usable company identity — LinkedIn, website and firmographics — without manual Google digging.

CRM data quality

Fill gaps on accounts that only have a razón social: official website, LinkedIn company page, domain, headcount and HQ. Typical CRM lists miss 30–60% of those fields before enrichment.

Campaigns & LinkedIn prospecting

Turn legal names into LinkedIn company URLs and web domains for list building, ABM, nurture and sales outreach — so marketing and SDR teams prospect from a verified firm page, not a guess.

Research & ops teams

Accelerate desk research, partner screening and market mapping when analysts need one consolidated record per company instead of five open tabs.

Vendor / supplier screening

Cross-check public presence (web + LinkedIn + HQ signals) before onboarding a supplier or account — with an explicit confidence score on every delivery.

Public data & compliance

We enrich from publicly available company signals (web + LinkedIn company pages), unify them and return one deliverable record with a confidence score.

  • We resolve firm identity from public web and LinkedIn company pages, then unify those signals into one deliverable record.
  • Matches can still be wrong. Deterministic scoring plus AI validation produce a confidence score so you can accept, review or reject each row.
  • One input name → one unified output record (LinkedIn, website, domain, firmographics and evidence), ready for CRM or campaign use.
  • Not a substitute for legal, KYC or compliance diligence — treat every match as assistive data to review.
