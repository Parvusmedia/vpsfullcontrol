What we do

A full ChatGPT Ads service, not a deck

Account and structure

We open or audit the Ads Manager account, set markets, budgets and objectives, and build the campaign → ad group → ad hierarchy the platform expects.

  • CPC, CPM or conversion bidding
  • Geo and platform targeting
  • Spend limits and launch checklist

Context, not keywords

ChatGPT does not buy on keyword lists. We write context hints and exclusions that match the questions your buyer already asks — that is what wins relevance in the auction.

  • Conversation research by category
  • Hint sets per ad group
  • Exclusions that protect the brand

Creative and measurement

Chat cards need a sharp title, a useful body, a fit image and a landing page that continues the comparison. We wire UTMs, pixels and conversion events so you can see what the click was worth.

  • Chat-card copy and assets
  • Landing-page brief for research-stage traffic
  • Weekly read of delivery and results

The placement

A labelled ad under the answer

Ads appear as sponsored cards in eligible ChatGPT conversations (Free and Go, in live markets). The user is already comparing options. Your job is to be the useful next step — not a banner they have to ignore.

  • Self-serve Ads Manager at ads.openai.com
  • No minimum spend on the self-serve path
  • Relevance-weighted auction: hints + bid + creative
